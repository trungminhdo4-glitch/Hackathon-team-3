# Vendor assessment pilot, 27 November 2026

The target was greater than 70% agreement on previously unseen real vendors. The best observed agreement in this pilot was 4/6 (66.7%). Use these cases only as development evidence from now on.

## Design

A fresh Terra worker selected the first six products in the displayed order of the [G2 2026 project management ranking](https://www.g2.com/best-software-companies/top-project-management) before researching their assessments. Submitted domains had zero matches among the 40 development URLs. The sample favors established products in a single category. Its two Green and four Yellow reference labels give no information on recall of Red cases.

The reference worker used a fixed procurement rubric and public evidence. A separate fresh Terra worker knew only the six products and its research instructions. It was instructed to seek institutional adoption and material counterevidence with a cap of three targeted searches and two decisive sources per product, excluding G2 and review rankings. Actual worker search counts and research wall time were not instrumented. Both workers were from the same model family. These are proxy judgments from one reviewer, without human arbitration. No organizer labels, submission endpoint or official time window were available to verify.

The production keyless batch ran once with collect/none/none. The research worker's findings were transcribed into the existing cache shape and passed to VERITAS public refetch and replay. This second path required a worker and manual orchestration. All predictions were saved and hashed before the reference worker released its labels. The evaluation-only CSV was created afterward. The scoring CLI addition did not change predictions.

Relative to keyless, one wrong decision became right and one right decision became wrong. Keyless matched one reference Yellow through its failure fallback, leaving 3/6 correct if failed research receives no credit. No variant predicted Red.

## What drove disagreements

* For Asana the researcher cited historical potential exposure through one feature. The reviewer judged the current product Green. The downloaded evidence was insufficient to settle applicability. The university PDF was unreadable to the text fetcher and the filing exceeded its byte limit.
* For monday AI Workspace the researcher found broad adoption and a resolved outage. The reviewer identified the specific AI product's credit-purchase requirement as material. This was an edition and pricing question the research missed. [Vendor pricing documentation](https://support.monday.com/hc/en-us/articles/35277848309394-The-pricing-model-for-monday-AI-portfolio)
* Of 12 cited reasons, 6 quotes matched retrieved text, 1 failed matching and 5 source pages could not be read. The failures comprised three responses rejected as unreadable public text, one size-limit rejection and one DNS failure. A matched quote alone does not establish that it entails a decisive claim. No independent entailment rate or evidence recall was measured.
* The guard kept a Green even though neither citation could be fetched. A preserved upstream verdict is not independent confirmation. Equal Yellow verdicts also sometimes had different rationales.

## Development evidence and patch

At base commit `171b921`, Shuhan's report on the two 27/40 development variants and the stored keyless reports (16/40 fetch, 17/40 collect) were available. The updated variant's 13 errors were 8 Green→Yellow, 4 Red→Yellow and 1 Yellow→Green. Per-vendor evidence was unavailable for a complete causal diagnosis of those errors. Shuhan's judge supplies anonymized labeled examples derived from development cases. A clean prospective comparison needs that prompt dependency removed and the configuration locked. No full live Shuhan rerun was performed.

Fetched evidence now follows its destination URL in verification, discovery and cached replay. A regression test exercises an apparent news citation redirecting to the vendor. On seven existing team audits the paired old/new comparison used identical page bytes and recorded the resolved URLs. Of 33 unique pages, 31 were readable, 11 changed URL and none of those changed registrable site. Both versions verified 18 of 25 checkable quotes, with one additional unreachable reason, and gave identical Guard verdicts (389 ms versus 399 ms offline replay). The provenance regression is corrected. Vendor accuracy gain attributable to this patch remains unmeasured.
