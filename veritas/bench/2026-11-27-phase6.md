### Focused recovery from already captured pages

A fresh Terra extractor saw only the three captured source documents and generic factual questions, without old verdicts or reference labels. It returned five complete passages, all checked against stored text. A fresh Terra judge then evaluated those packets with the frozen rubric: Yellow, Yellow, Red, **1/3 correct**. Compared with the default Guard's three Yellows this is +1/0; compared with the upstream three Reds it is 0/+2 losses. No new research calls, two worker calls. Extraction recorded 5 seconds; judgment 27 seconds. This is a post-hoc selected diagnostic, not a full-cohort accuracy increase. Recovery output is judge-self-assessed, not included in the separate 40-case semantic audit.

The result establishes a practical improvement to evidence retention, but also demonstrates a remaining **judgment/materiality bottleneck**. More complete evidence does not guarantee agreement with procurement reference judgments. Do not promote the recovered three cases into an alleged 40-case improvement or hardcode their answers.

## IMPLEMENTED

- `evidence-packet.js`: field-whitelisted packets without prior decisions; exact available passage/context separated from unverified discovery leads. Generated quotations never become source content.
- `refetchDocuments`: bounded actual retrieved text, original/final/canonical URL, actual timestamp, locally computed SHA-256 over stored text, truncation and retrieval provenance. The hash detects artifact changes; a worker-supplied hash is not proof of retrieval. Existing fetch safety remains in place.
- `replay --research-cache`: reuse captured text with no refetch. Existing imported audits still need retrieval to verify missing content.
- Opt-in `--guard-policy audit-only`: original verdict, proposed reassessment and `independently_verified_overall: null` stay distinct. Nondecisive missing reasons do not by themselves change a supported upstream label. It does not certify semantic support. Forty-case replay retains 21/40 upstream colors, requests reassessment for 37/40, and certifies zero independent overall verdicts. **No grounded accuracy improvement is claimed.** Existing and strict Guard defaults are not enabled for competition by this work.
- Offline paired/crossover/native-pilot scoring and immutable packet hashes; separate evaluation artifacts from production worker inputs. No new dependencies, classifier, crawler, frontend or orchestration framework.

The audit issue handling is operationally: unreachable source → unknown/access failure; missing or unmatched quote → invalid quotation or missing passage; matched but wrong product → scope problem; matched but non-entailing passage → unsupported/partial proposition; suitable actual counter-evidence → contradiction. None of the first four proves the underlying allegation false. Recover decisive passages or reassess. First-party technical, pricing and lifecycle sources remain usable for facts they can establish. Independent allegations/adoption require suitable stronger evidence.
